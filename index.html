About Me

My scientific journey began with a passion for understanding how biological systems function and how data can be transformed into knowledge capable of generating real-world impact.

I hold a PhD and a Master's degree in Plant Biotechnology from UENF (State University of Northern Rio de Janeiro), where I specialized in transcriptomics, functional genomics, and bioinformatics applied to crop improvement.

Over the past years, I have worked with large-scale biological datasets, including RNA-seq, SNPs, co-expression networks, and comparative genomics. My research has focused primarily on identifying genes associated with agronomic traits such as oil accumulation, stress tolerance, and productivity in economically important crops.

Beyond research, I am deeply interested in scientific communication and education. I have developed educational materials, outreach projects, workshops, and digital content aimed at making bioinformatics and biotechnology more accessible to diverse audiences.
